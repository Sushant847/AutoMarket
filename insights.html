Market insights

Patterns from the Australian vehicle dataset — useful before you start shortlisting.

Which brands have the most listings?

Based on the loaded dataset. More listings often means more choice when comparing prices.

How are fuel types distributed?

Helps you see how common petrol, diesel and hybrid options are in this data.

Which body types are most common?

SUVs, hatchbacks and utes dominate many Australian used-car searches.

How does average price change by year?

In this dataset, newer model years generally show higher average asking prices.

How to use these insights

  • Use brand and body-type charts to see where you have the most choice.
  • Use the year–price chart to set a realistic budget for the age of car you want.
  • Fuel-type mix can affect running costs — factor that in before you buy.
  • These are patterns in the dataset, not guarantees about any single car.
